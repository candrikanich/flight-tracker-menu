# Sky log (issue #5)

**Goal:** remember every plane the card showed so you can look back ("what was that loud one at 6:40?").

**Decided with Chris:** keep 30 days, no CSV export, nothing leaves the Mac.

## Design
- **FlightKit `SkyLog.swift`** (pure, tested)
  - `Pass` (Codable): hex, callsign, registration, type, operator, route, phase, squawk, first/last seen, closest distance, lowest height.
  - `SkyLog.record(_ sighting:)` merges sightings of the same plane into one pass while the gaps stay ≤ `FlightTracker.reannounceAfter` (120 s). Otherwise it starts a new pass. A route found later fills in a pass that had none. It prunes passes older than 30 days.
  - `SkyLog.days(calendar:)` groups passes by day, newest first; `Formatting.dayTitle` gives "Today", "Yesterday" or "Tue, Nov 10".
  - `Airport`, `Route` and `FlightPhase` become Codable.
- **FlightTracker** owns `skyLog`: it records each `current` sighting and offers `restore(_:)` at launch.
- **App**
  - `SkyLogStore` reads and writes `Application Support/Overflight/sky-log.json` atomically.
  - It saves when a new pass starts, at most once a minute otherwise, and on quit.
  - A corrupt file is moved aside instead of overwritten.
- **UI**
  - A "Sky Log" window: sections by day, each row shows time, flight, type, route and closest/lowest.
  - Clicking a row expands its details.
  - The card's footer gets a "Sky log" button.

## Scope notes
- Only the plane the card shows (the nearest qualifying one) is logged, matching what you saw.
- The daily tally (#2) and flight-path map (#6) build on this later.

## Review follow-ups
- Fixed: the tracker passes `startsNewPass` into the log, so one flight isn't split when another plane is closer for a while.
- Fixed: a trailing save lands within a minute after the last change.
- Fixed: restore prunes passes older than 30 days.
- Fixed: unreadable files are kept under timestamped names, and only a missing file counts as a first launch.
- Deferred: JSON encoding and writing happen on the main actor. That's fine at today's size; move them to a background task if the log grows.
- Deferred: one undecodable pass discards the whole file (moved aside). This would only matter after a downgrade across a schema change.
